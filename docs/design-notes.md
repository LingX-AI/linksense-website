# Design adaptation

The `qclay` preview adapts the public [QClay homepage](https://qclay.design/), inspected at 1280×720 on November 23, 2026. LinkSense keeps its own branding, Inter typeface, product claims, first-party footage, and six localized versions. QClay is visual evidence only; no QClay assets, copy, fonts, or implementation code are used.

| Area | LinkSense adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| Header | The wordmark sits in normal page flow at the page margin rather than in a sticky pill. Wide, centered text links use the existing routes and documentation. The right side pairs a thin outlined demo pill with a lime dot and a lowercase `menu` control drawn with two strokes. The theme control moves into the menu so the header stays close to the reference. |
| Menu | A native modal `<dialog>` opens a roomy paper sheet with a short diagonal reveal. Small utilities (docs, GitHub, demo, a three-way Light/System/Dark theme switch, six languages) sit left, a portrait crop of the owned menu artwork sits center, and large numbered page links sit right. Escape, the close button, and a press-and-release outside the sheet dismiss it; the browser returns focus to the trigger. The reverse animation is skipped for reduced motion. |
| Homepage introduction | A three-line, left-aligned narrative sits high on the near-white canvas. A compact source/platform/data row, description, and outlined self-hosting pill sit below, followed by open space before the 16:9 film. |
| Film and workspace | The supplied interlocking-link artwork covers the film and the menu sheet. The film opens on demand in the existing native player with a direct media fallback. The workspace still and demo retain the example procurement deliverables and the established `#workspace` / `#deliverables` anchors. |
| Work sequence | The homepage continues as numbered editorial chapters: workspace, use cases, sharing, and ownership. Media is full-width or paired in offset diptychs; the former repeated use-case cards are replaced by a typographic index plus real product screens. |
| Use cases | The route reads as an asymmetric project sequence. Procurement pairs the real workspace capture with the deliverable artwork; reporting and knowledge alternate offset artifacts and full-width fields. All three real example IDs, output copy, and the existing sharing spread remain. |
| Self-hosted | Core and Full use different compositions rather than a matching card grid: Core is a wide outlined chapter with a ring graphic, while Full is an offset tinted panel with layered artwork. Governance keeps the existing deployment illustration and controls in its own chalk chapter; boundaries close on a distinct ring-and-copy spread. |
| Narrow screens | The same narrative, menu order, chapters, and media sequence stack without a separate mobile theme. The menu sheet scrolls internally and keeps its utility, artwork, and primary-link groups. |
| Theme and motion | The persisted light/dark override and system default are unchanged. Movement is limited to short menu, hover, and focus feedback; the cover and artwork stay static under `prefers-reduced-motion`. |

## Media

The workspace demo (`product-demo.mp4`), its hover clip, and its poster are a code-built motion reconstruction of the real workspace captures (source project: `~/c/linksense-motion`); the two feature hover loops (`skills-preview.mp4`, `connections-preview.mp4`) come from `~/c/linksense-motion-skills` and `~/c/linksense-motion-connections`. The interfaces are traced from the captures, and all copy comes from the product capture or this site. The demo's score is generated, quiet electronic; the hover loops are silent by design; no spoken dialogue.
- The introduction film has English on-screen text. Its music is **Sonor #2**, Eugenio Mininni, under the [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/).
- `workspace-result.webp` is a frame from the end of `product-demo.mp4`. `public/media/qclay-links.webp` is the supplied interlocking-link artwork, adapted to 16:9 for the film and cropped to portrait in the menu sheet. No QClay assets, copy, or implementation code are used.
- Deliverable illustrations use example data. The previous blue signal artwork (`signal.webp`) is no longer referenced by the current pages.
