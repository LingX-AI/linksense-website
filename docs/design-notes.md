# Design adaptation

The `exoape` preview takes its visual direction from the [Exo Ape homepage](https://www.exoape.com/), using the Bryce Nice Site Creator profile and its October 16, 2026 REA evidence. LinkSense retains its own brand, Inter typeface, product copy, first-party product media, and six localized versions of its three pages.

| Area | LinkSense adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| First visit | A tab-session curtain shows the LinkSense L glyph from the real lockup geometry, fills it bottom-to-top, then tilts the opaque curtain away. The hero heading and supporting copy wait for that reveal. The cue never waits for media; no-JS, reduced motion, unavailable storage, failed hydration and a bounded timeout all leave the page visible. Reloads and internal route changes do not replay it. |
| Hero | An original full-bleed waterfront scene frames large negative space, a short supporting paragraph in the lower-middle left, and an oversized thin title anchored at the bottom. Titles and copy enter in staggered, tilted masks after the curtain clears. The image drifts with native scroll where supported and follows the pointer with a small damped parallax on fine pointers; portrait layouts crop toward the tower. CJK locales use a slightly smaller display size and looser leading so the authored lines do not collide. |
| Header | A floating wordmark and one lightweight Menu entry sit directly over the opening frame, with no bar, row, toolbar or compact chrome. Page, documentation, GitHub, demo, theme and language controls live in the full-screen dialog and the footer; the footer also carries the in-page links as a no-script fallback. |
| Projects | A sparse, asymmetric three-project field replaces the former four-panel showcase. Run, deliverables and share occupy deliberately unequal 12-column placements; the deliverables still is tilted and straightens on hover. The procurement preview autoplays while visible, the skills preview plays on hover, and fine-pointer captions collapse to a single line until hover or focus. Touch and reduced motion keep the supporting copy visible. |
| Reel and closing | The dark introduction reel is the only other major beat before the footer: it pins on sufficiently wide and tall screens with scroll-timeline support, the full-bleed product still expands, and the two title lines converge separately over a cinematic scrim. Smaller viewports use a compact stacked chapter. The closing is a full-bleed art-directed call to action. The former homepage use-case index, governance overview and portrait closing grid are gone; their full detail remains on the use cases and self-hosting routes. |
| Detail pages | The use cases open with a project cover, then use numbered editorial spreads with differently scaled product stills, illustrative outputs, and a dark sharing feature. Self-hosting opens with a cover and treats Core as an open editorial column beside the offset Full edition panel, followed by a dark deployment and security spread. Both keep the existing fragment destinations and deployment links. |
| Navigation and theme | The native dialog reveals diagonally in both directions and uses one supplied glass-link artwork as its preview. It is reachable at every viewport width. Same-document link transitions use the browser's native snapshots where supported; the menu counter-motion and route transition keep separate transform owners. Back/Forward keeps normal restoration; reduced motion skips the cinematic transitions. Theme selection follows the system unless explicitly overridden. |

These are adaptations, not a frame-for-frame remake: there is no virtual scroll controller or extra studio, project, or news routes. A fine-pointer-only translucent lag cursor is scoped to the project field; it is disabled for touch and reduced motion, and native focus remains visible.

## Media

- The header/footer lockups and the app icon come from the LinkSense logo Figma file (`Linksense-logo设计`, node 80:2) and are kept as `public/brand/linksense-lockup-light.svg`, `linksense-lockup-dark.svg` and `linksense-icon.svg`. The first-visit curtain reuses the `Glyph-1-l` path from the lockup SVG rather than drawing a new L. See the [brand policy](../../linksense/TRADEMARK.md).
- The introduction film is reused from the [existing website's first-party media](../../linksense-site/public/media/README.md). The workspace demo (`product-demo.mp4`), its hover clip, and its poster are a code-built motion reconstruction of the real workspace captures (source project: `~/c/linksense-motion`); the two feature hover loops (`skills-preview.mp4`, `connections-preview.mp4`) come from `~/c/linksense-motion-skills` and `~/c/linksense-motion-connections`. The interfaces are traced from the captures, and all copy comes from the product capture or this site. The demo's score is generated, quiet electronic; the hover loops are silent by design; no spoken dialogue.
- The introduction film has English on-screen text. Its music is **Sonor #2**, Eugenio Mininni, under the [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/).
- `workspace-teaser.mp4` is a shortened, silent version of `workspace-preview.mp4`; `workspace-result.webp` is a frame from the end of `product-demo.mp4`. `public/media/exoape-hero.webp` is an original procedural rendering for this preview; it does not reuse Exo Ape's photography or code.
- Deliverable illustrations use example data. The full-screen menu uses the supplied glass-link artwork.
- The bundled Inter font includes its [OFL license](../public/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt).
